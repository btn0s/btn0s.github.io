Privacy Policy

Best Roblox Clips Publisher. Effective November 17, 2026.

Best Roblox Clips Publisher ("the app") is a private tool the owner of the YouTube channel Best Roblox Clips uses to publish and measure that channel's own videos. Only the channel's owner signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the permissions granted at sign-in (YouTube manage and upload, YouTube Analytics read-only), the app accesses only the signed-in channel's data:

  • the channel's videos and playlists, and their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy and schedule settings
  • uploads of the channel's own new videos
  • the channel's and its videos' statistics and analytics (views, watch time, retention)

It does not access other users' data, comments, subscribers' personal information, or any Google service other than YouTube.

How the data is used

Only to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, keep their details and playlists up to date, and decide which kinds of videos to make next based on their performance.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token and the analytics the app reads are stored only on the channel owner's own computers. They are not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted, and stored analytics are deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
